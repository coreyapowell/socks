============================================================
   HOW TO SEE YOUR USAGE OF CHATS AND PREMIUM REQUESTS
============================================================

1. Initialize the library:
   sock.init();
✓ Socket library initialized

2. Use your application (track your usage):
   // Every time you make a chat request:
   sock.trackChatRequest();
   // Every time you make a premium request:
   sock.trackPremiumRequest();
   // Connections and data transfer are tracked automatically

3. 🎯 SEE YOUR USAGE (this answers your question!):
   let stats = sock.getUsageStats();

========================================
         YOUR CURRENT USAGE
========================================
📱 Chat requests: 3
⭐ Premium requests: 2
🔗 Total connections: 1
📊 Data transferred: 1024 bytes sent, 512 bytes received
========================================

4. You can also format it however you like:
   console.log(`You have made ${stats.chatRequests} chat requests`);
   console.log(`You have made ${stats.premiumRequests} premium requests`);

   ➜ You have made 3 chat requests
   ➜ You have made 2 premium requests

5. Get detailed breakdown:
   ➜ Total requests: 5
   ➜ Premium usage: 40.0% of your requests
   ➜ Data usage: 1024.50 KB total

6. Optional: Reset your usage counters:
   sock.resetUsageStats();

============================================================
🎉 THAT'S IT! You now know how to see your usage!
============================================================

To use this in your actual application:
1. Import the compiled socks library: import * as sock from './sock.so'
2. Call sock.trackChatRequest() when you make chat requests
3. Call sock.trackPremiumRequest() when you make premium requests
4. Call sock.getUsageStats() to see your current usage

The library automatically tracks connections and data transfer for you!
